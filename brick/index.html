Brick Game

Just like the real thing: press ON/OFF, then pick a cartridge with ← →, a speed with ↑ ↓, a level with ROTATE, and hit S/P to start.

    Notes

    • Speed is how fast things move; level changes the challenge (Tetris starts with garbage rows, Snake gets obstacles, Breaker gets more bricks…). In Tetris, ↑ is a hard drop.
    • Hold ROTATE in Snake or Road Fighter for a turbo boost.
    • Tap CLASSIC under the screen to change the LCD tint. High scores are saved on this device.
    • On iPhone the ringer switch silences web audio.
