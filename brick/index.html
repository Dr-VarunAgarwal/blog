Title keys D-pad on the title screen

The D-pad on the title screen, where you pick game, speed and level. Original: LEFT level, RIGHT speed, DOWN/UP game (as printed on the real units). Quick: LEFT/RIGHT game, UP/DOWN speed, ROTATE level.

Reset

Refresh clears the saved copy of the page and loads the latest version (your skin and scores stay). Use it if anything looks half-updated. Erase also deletes your skin, colours and high scores.

Just like the real thing: press ON/OFF, pick a game, speed and level on the title screen (see Title keys under Play), and hit S/P to start.
